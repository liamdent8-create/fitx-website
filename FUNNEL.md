## 1. The whole journey

```
ATTENTION            CLICK                 LANDING PAGE                      BUY             AFTER THE BUY                 THE REAL SALE
Instagram content →  link in bio / ad →    Hook → Trust → Proof → Offer  →  $89 trial on  →  same-day DM/text from Loz → Premium $75/wk
Ambassadors' posts   ManyChat keyword      → Urgency → Risk → CTA (×6+)     Mindbody         workshop + first classes      by day 14
Meta ads (cold)      DM link                                                                  day 7 check-in DM
Warm outreach                                                                                 day 12 "stay on" conversation
```

- **Content** (Lauren, @fitx.au, the four ambassadors, EBT-style education) makes people *warm*. Hormozi's point: each platform has its own audience, so content doesn't "funnel" from Shorts to long videos. It funnels from content to the profile, then to the link. So the landing page has to work for someone arriving from Instagram who has *already seen Loz* (warm), and for someone from a Meta ad who hasn't (cold).
- **The landing page's one job** is to get the $89 purchase. Brunson: every step is Hook → Story → Offer, and when a funnel leaks it's one of those three.
- **The money is in the step after the page.** The $89 is the front of the value ladder: apparel → $89 trial → Premium $75/wk (or online coaching). Hormozi's "hack to convert more leads": someone whose only job is to contact a new lead the minute they come in. For FIT X, that means Loz (or a ManyChat flow) messages every trial buyer the same day.

## 4. What makes it "Apple" rather than just "EBT"

EBT gives us the order. Apple gives us the finish:
- Big rounded tiles (≈28px corners) on soft grey, with the image filling the tile, a small label over a short title, and a round **+** that opens a detail sheet. The sheet grows out of the tile you tapped, follows your finger, and closes with a swipe down (velocity and rubber-banding, like iOS).
- Headings in a semibold weight with tight tracking. Promo titles in the condensed italic of the FIT X logotype.
- Section headers with a right-aligned "See all ›" link. Carousels with round chevron buttons.
- Frosted glass for anything floating over content (nav, dock, sheet, contact panel).
- Motion that's critically damped by default, never bouncy unless you flicked it. Everything can be interrupted, and reduced-motion users get cross-fades.
