# FIT X Studio brand guidelines

One page for anyone making a page, ad, post or Canva design. The code version of these rules is in `src/styles/global.css`.

## Colour: two quiet, one loud

| Role | Colour | Hex | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet 1 | Navy | `#0D1B36` | Headings, dark sections, the header bar, text on volt |
| Quiet 2 | White / mist | `#FFFFFF` / `#F5F5F7` | Backgrounds and cards |
| **Loud** | **Volt** | **`#D4FF3D`** | **Only things we want clicked or noticed first: the $89 trial button, the $89 price, the offer banner, "Best value"** |
| Support | Slate (logo tile) | `#556280` | Small accents, never buttons |
| Support | Ice (neon sign) | `#A9DCFF` | Ticks and outlines on navy only |

Rules:
- Volt text always sits on navy, and navy text always sits on volt. Never put white text on volt, or volt text on white.
- One volt thing per screen where possible. If everything is loud, nothing is.
- No purple-to-blue gradients and no glowing orbs.

## Type

| Use | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines, offers | Anton (the logo face) | Uppercase, leaning like the logo |
| Everything else | System font (SF on iPhone) | Sentence case |

- Short headlines: 2 to 6 words. Examples: "Choose how you train.", "What members say."
- Body copy at a grade 5 reading level. Australian English.

## Voice

- Outcome first: what she gets, then how.
- Specific, not hype: "A coach shows you each lift" beats "elevate your fitness journey".
- No em dashes in marketing copy, no "it's not X, it's Y", no emojis in headings.
- Women only, beginner friendly, never body-shaming. No before-and-after photos and no negative body framing.
- Every price and claim must be true and on the page: real prices from Mindbody and the fee schedule, real reviews word for word.

## Photos and video

- Real studio photos only; no stock. People photos need consent (see the privacy policy, section 11).
- Best next asset: a 6 to 10 second silent loop from the VSL footage (women training, the neon sign) for the top of the studio page.

## Buttons and links

- Say exactly what happens: "Claim my 2 weeks", "Try 2 weeks for $89", "Book a class". Never just "Learn more" or "Submit".
- Trial buttons are volt. Everything else is quiet (navy, white or a text link).

## Logo

- White logo on navy; navy logo on white. Files are in `public/brand/`.
- Square tile (slate) for profile pictures and the browser tab icon.
